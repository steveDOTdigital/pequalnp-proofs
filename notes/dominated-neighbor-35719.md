# Restricting the NOT source for L-meta-35719

The monotone control uses 15 gates. The signed replays exclude 11 paid binary gates after setting vertex 0 or vertex 2 to zero. Reflection carries the vertex-0 bound to vertex 1. These three restrictions need at least 12 paid binary gates with free complementation. [Lean control and reflection](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/96c060f143cdde0bc2813019b64dc1f1422f2883/notes/EightVertexOneNot35719.lean) · [Vertex-0 replay](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/0825124bdd270ebb46f55e3809b9bfc569c05afc/notes/signed-pin-zero-35719.cjs) · [Vertex-2 replay](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/ad3542ce2c10697d856bb1d7210ef7a8975342d2/notes/signed-pin-two-35719.cjs)

Suppose one NOT gives an improvement. Choose a smallest improving circuit and then minimize the number of pairs consisting of a live OR gate and a later AND or NOT gate that it can reach. Remove dead gates, constants, copies and repeated operands wherever they simplify the circuit. The circuit has at most 13 binary gates and each of the three marked inputs is essential.

For a marked input p, setting p to zero simplifies every direct binary reader of p. Two such readers would leave at most 11 paid gates. At least one binary reader is necessary: dependence solely through the NOT would make the output nonincreasing in this essential positive input. The NOT cannot read p directly as well. In that case its constant value after the pin simplifies a binary consumer, distinct from the sole direct binary reader unless a gate combines p and its complement and already simplifies. The sole binary reader of p must be OR. An AND becomes constant on the zero pin, and a live downstream binary gate then also simplifies. Passing through the single NOT still transmits a constant. The restricted output is nonconstant, so the constant branch cannot end at the output without reaching another binary gate.

For the sole reader p OR W, the sibling W is independent of p. If W implies the output, bypass the reader by p and append an OR with W at the output. On W=false the old evaluator agrees with the bypass, and on W=true both outputs are true. There is a path from the removed gate to an AND or NOT. Otherwise the path to the output would propagate the singleton input p=true through OR gates and accept a nonedge. The bypass removes this OR ancestor and introduces no new OR ancestor of an AND or NOT. After simplification this improves the chosen minimum, or gives a monotone circuit at the same size. Accordingly W is true on some negative input. One can take p=false, since W is independent of p and the graph predicate is monotone. Turning p on at that same input keeps the output false, because W already saturates the only reader. [Lean verification of OR relocation](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/9877e9b94354ae6d08ee4e4246837f25b2b39a03/notes/TwinLift35289.lean)

For p=0 use its neighbor z=3. Apart from 0, the neighbors of 3 are 5 and 6, both neighbors of 0. For p=1 use z=4. Apart from 1, the neighbors of 4 are 6 and 7, both neighbors of 1. At the negative input found above, no neighbor of p is true. Turn z on. The row stays negative with p=false and becomes positive with p=true. The sibling W must therefore fall from true to false upon turning z on. Lean proves this independent-set argument for any neighbor with the same containment property. [Lean witness lemma](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/3bfe67c608da2ac222aff660602cb8c872f98782/notes/DominatedNeighbor35719.lean)

Along each such row pair, all primary inputs and all pre-NOT wires are nondecreasing. If the source h of the NOT did not rise on the pair, the NOT output would be constant and every later wire would still be nondecreasing. Thus h rises across a quiet-to-exposed row for z=3 and across one for z=4. Both marked OR readers are after the NOT, so h is independent of inputs 0 and 1. In particular, no raw input can be the source of the lone NOT in the selected smallest improvement.

The searches enforce these restrictions on the actual AND/OR/NOT evaluator. The 12-gate signed search on the vertex-2 restriction hit its limit. The graph claim still requires an exact lower bound or a completed exclusion of the remaining NOT sources.
