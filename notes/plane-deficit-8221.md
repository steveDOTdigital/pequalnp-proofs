# A stronger packing bound and exact incidence in the degree-two case

The [desk assignment L-meta-8221](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-8221.2937b9a70837.json) allows both syntactic degrees to exceed one. If both are exactly two, any circuit that accepts all flags and rejects every capped independent pair computes the full incidence function. An accepting independent pair has a smaller accepted pair with at most two selected points and two selected lines, by recursive circuit support. That smaller pair is automatically capped. The resulting contradiction excludes every independent input. Any input containing a flag is accepted because it contains the support of that flag input. Lean verifies the argument using the project circuit definitions and the posted `Fin.addCases` input representation. [Lean proof](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/c25e107bbd285ee80698a193488b173e31fb016b/notes/PlaneExactTwo8221.lean)

## Ruling out 51 omissions in the quadrangle test family

In the [point-degree-two, line-degree-three construction](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/c9b5dfc8d58e7729e9ddaa3b43e25a8079d91593/notes/plane-degree-three-7915.md), omitted quadrangles share no triangle. Let (c_{ab}) be the number of omissions containing the pair of lines (a,b). At most four omissions contain a pair because each consumes two of its nine nonconcurrent triples. Put (d_{ab}=4-c_{ab}).

If there are 51 omissions, the total pair deficit is six, since each quadrangle contains six pairs. At any line the sum of incident deficits is divisible by three. Each omission through that line uses exactly three of its incident pairs, and the total allowance there is 48. Thus the nonzero deficit graph has total degree 12 and at most four vertices. It cannot use only two vertices, since an edge has weight at most four. With three vertices the degrees are (6,3,3), forcing a two-edge star of weight three on each edge. With four vertices each degree is three. Comparing equations at opposite pairs of vertices shows the edge weights of each opposite pair are equal. The three weights of the perfect matchings therefore sum to three, with possibilities ((3,0,0),(2,1,0),(1,1,1)) up to permutation.

The replay enumerates all 8,008 labeled patterns of those types and verifies incidence preservation for 5,616 collineations generated by images of a frame. The patterns fall into eleven orbits. For each representative, the search enumerates exactly the allowed choices of quadrangles through each line pair. It propagates forced decisions, branches on both values of an undecided quadrangle, and rules out all eleven. A separate exhaustive search without symmetry rules out 52 omissions, confirming the result of two SAT encodings. [Deficit-orbit replay](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/f14c5854585b6939bf50be429d380f365a919d6a/notes/plane-deficit-8221.cjs) · [Pair-saturation encoding](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/0aa134cffe9e5b851b4199cb26959ff595320140/notes/plane-pair-saturation-8221.cjs)

Thus at least 184 quadrangle tests are required for this particular inclusion-cover construction. The checked construction uses 186. These bounds do not settle the number of circuit gates.
