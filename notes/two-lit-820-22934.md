# Six gates for the two-lit graph 820, with or without NOT

On the promise that one or two of the six pins are lit, the accepted pairs are {0,2}, {0,4}, {0,5}, {1,2} and {1,3}. Singletons are rejected. The output is the OR of two terms: pin 0 with any of pins 2, 4 and 5, or pin 1 with either pin 2 or pin 3. This uses six binary gates. Lean checked every ordered pair of pins, including all six singletons. [Lean proof](TwoLit820Exact.lean)

The proof establishes three distinct catalogue statements.

| Catalogue claim | Independent result |
| --- | --- |
| [L-meta-22934](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-22934.8d9952d9ff2e.json), five AND/OR gates suffice | Disproved by `monotoneLower`. |
| [L-meta-21901](https://pequalnp.com/lemma#L-meta-21901), six AND/OR gates suffice | Proved by `catalogue21901`. |
| [L-meta-22766](https://pequalnp.com/lemma#L-meta-22766), five gates suffice when NOT is allowed | Disproved by `generalLower`. |

For the monotone lower bound, the remaining k gates can use at most k+1 previously computed wires. Each accepted/rejected comparison needs a wire true on the first input and false on the second. The finite certificate gives k+2 comparisons that no one wire can separate twice, or splits by the next gate when one certificate does not yet suffice. Lean checks both splits, all 83 exclusions and the circuit semantics.

For the stronger lower bound, select six singleton-to-pair comparisons, one per input pin. In each comparison the output changes and only that pin changes. Each input wire therefore distinguishes exactly one comparison. A NOT gate copies the set of comparisons distinguished by its first operand. A binary gate can distinguish only comparisons distinguished by one of its two operands. Tracing any selected set of wires back through such a gate can add at most one wire. Hence an output distinguishing all six needs at least five gates other than NOT. If a circuit of at most five gates contains a NOT, the count is too small. A circuit with no NOT is ruled out by the monotone proof. This argument includes default reads, constants and unused gates.

Lean reported only the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound for the lower bounds, and propext for the construction. At the time of verification the desk listed three open filings for 21901 and supplied no negated filing for 22934.
