# Six gates for graph 314, with or without NOT

On the six-input two-lit promise, this graph accepts four pairs incident to vertex 0, namely {0, 1}, {0, 3}, {0, 4} and {0, 5}. Its fifth edge is {1, 2}. All six singletons are rejected.

The circuit computes `(x₀ ∧ (x₁ ∨ x₃ ∨ x₄ ∨ x₅)) ∨ (x₁ ∧ x₂)` in six binary gates. It agrees with the specified graph on all 36 ordered pairs. Lean also ruled out every circuit of at most five gates, even allowing NOT and shared intermediate wires. The independent search over live wires reached 1, 3, 1 and 0 states with five down to two gates remaining. The certificate checked all legal transitions against the project's circuit semantics.
