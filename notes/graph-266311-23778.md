Each support certificate has two parts. For a suffix of at most `n` monotone gates, pick `n+2` ordered pairs where the target falls from true to false. Every selected pair must have a starting wire that also falls, and a certificate checks that no wire can serve two pairs. For this case, `n+1` pairs that need distinct distinguishing wires suffice, and either direction of change counts.
