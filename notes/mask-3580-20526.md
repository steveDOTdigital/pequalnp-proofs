# Nine monotone gates for the three-by-five graph 3580

The [desk claim L-meta-20526](https://pequalnp.com/lemma#L-meta-20526) asks for eight binary AND/OR gates. The minimum is **nine**. In column order 0 to 4 the neighborhoods are {2,3,4}, {0,1,2,3} and {0,1}. Define X as c0 OR c1 and Y as c2 OR c3. Compute the OR of three terms, X AND (r1 OR r2), Y AND (r0 OR r1), and r0 AND c4. The two column ORs, two row ORs, three ANDs and two output ORs give nine gates. The calculation reproduces 3580 on all fifteen promised inputs.

Every raw indicator must occur in the live output cone. Omitting an indicator makes a switch from that vertex to any other vertex on its side an increase in the observed coordinates. The omitted vertex would have to have a neighborhood contained in every other neighborhood on its side. There is no such row or column. The replay checks an increasing pair with a falling answer for each of the eight proposed omissions. Invalid indexes, empty operations and unary gates can be folded as constants or copies for this nonconstant target.

Let b denote the number of live binary gates. A connected output cone with eight distinct raw sources and b gates has 2b input edges and 8+b vertices, so b≥7. If b=7 the cone is a tree. More generally, whenever no internal result is shared, the circuit is a formula, possibly with repeated raw occurrences. The replay computes the tables of minimum formula cost by combining smaller formulas using AND and OR. No formula through eight gates computes 3580.

It remains to consider b=8 and a shared internal wire. Count uses of the non-output wires. Each must be used at least once and the 16 gate inputs leave room for only one extra use. Consequently exactly one internal wire is used twice, all the other internal wires are used once and each raw wire is used once. The shared wire is a read-once tree on a subset S of k raw indicators. It costs k−1 gates. Above it the circuit is a formula on the remaining raw indicators and that shared value, of size at most 9−k. If k=8 the suffix can only return the shared value or combine it with itself, already covered by the formula case.

For each 2≤k≤7, enumerate read-once tables by splitting the support into two nonempty disjoint parts and combining their tables. Treat the shared table as an available input alongside the other raw indicators. If any accepted cell has its input signals contained in the signals of a rejected cell, no monotone formula on those signals can suffice. Of 36,117 support/table choices, ten passed this test. For all ten, the formula calculation excluded the remaining budget. This exhausts all live circuit shapes at the claimed size.

The [standalone finite check](mask-3580-20526.py) verifies the upper bound, essential inputs, formula cases and shared-wire count. A separate JavaScript implementation gave the same ten survivors and excluded all of them. The finite lower-bound calculation has not been certified in Lean.
