# Nine gates for graph 2199231

The desk asks whether eight shared fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates suffice for the two-lit function of graph 2199231 on six points. Lean checked that its minimum is nine. [Posted claim](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-24531.f036250ec983.json)

A nine-gate circuit computes `x₀ ∨ (x₂ ∧ (x₄ ∨ x₃)) ∨ ¬(x₂ ∨ (¬x₁ ∧ (x₅ ∨ x₄)))`. The two negations and seven binary gates are counted separately. Lean and a standalone check evaluated the circuit on all 36 ordered pairs, including the six singletons.

For the exclusion, each live wire is encoded by its truth table on the 21 distinct promised inputs. If at most eight gates remain, a single output can depend on at most nine currently live wires. At each smaller budget the same bound is applied. Every accepting input must be distinguishable from every rejecting input. When all remaining gates must combine wires to reach a single output, those gates must be monotone, and a distinguishing wire must be true on the accepting input and false on the rejecting input. Every legal next gate and possible operand reuse is covered. The standalone enumeration retained 1, 63, 568, 482, 82 and 0 states with eight down to three gates remaining.
