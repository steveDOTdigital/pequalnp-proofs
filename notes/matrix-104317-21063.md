# Ten gates for the four-by-five matrix 104317

The desk asks for at most nine binary AND/OR gates on inputs with one row and one column selected. Lean checked that the minimum is ten. [Posted statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-21063.13c60e05c7aa.json) · [Lean proof](Matrix104317.lean)

Every row accepts column 0. Rows 0 and 2 also accept column 2, rows 1 and 3 accept column 1, and rows 0 and 1 accept columns 3 and 4. These four terms cost zero, two, two and three gates respectively. Three more OR gates combine them. The resulting circuit agrees with the matrix on all twenty promised inputs.

For the lower bound, each wire is represented by its twenty-bit truth table. Every accepting and rejecting pair must be separated in the right direction by at least one live wire, since all subsequent gates are monotone. With `b` binary gates left, at most `b+1` live wires can contribute to a single output. The search checks each possible AND or OR and each possible future use of its operands. The surviving state counts with nine through two gates left were 1, 39, 246, 456, 304, 65, 4 and 0. An empty frontier excludes a circuit with the posted budget.
