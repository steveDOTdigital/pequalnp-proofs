# Eight gates for graph 34087

[Graph34087.lean](Graph34087.lean) proves that graph 34087 needs exactly eight fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates on six inputs. This resolves two catalogue claims. [L-meta-23274](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-23274.f2399fa43943.json) asks whether seven suffice. [L-meta-22573](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-22573.4c43cdd9c602.json) asks whether eight suffice.

## The circuit

Number the six inputs from 0 through 5 and the gates from 6 through 13. In order, compute `OR(0,3)`, `OR(2,4)`, `AND(1,7)`, `OR(3,7)`, `NOT(9)`, `OR(2,10)`, `AND(6,11)` and `OR(8,12)`. The output is gate 13. Lean checks all 36 ordered pairs, including each singleton. The accepted unordered inputs are {0}, {0,1}, {0,2}, {0,5}, {1,2}, {1,4} and {2,3}.

## Why seven gates cannot suffice

For the lower bound, consider only the fifteen singleton and pair inputs on pins 0 through 4. Pin 5 is always false on this restriction. Represent each wire by its fifteen output bits. Starting from the output, trace which wire values are needed before each gate. If a needed value comes from the gate, replace it with its operands. If another value is used again, keep it in the set. The proof establishes this backward construction for every circuit under the project's semantics, including constants, repeated operands and default reads.

With `n` gates left, at most `n+1` wire values are needed. At least one wire must distinguish every accepted input from every rejected input. If exactly `n+1` wires are needed, all remaining gates must combine two wires to reach one output. In that case some needed wire must be true on the accepted input and false on the rejected input. The kernel uses these necessary conditions to discard impossible sets.

On the restricted inputs, the numbers of viable reachable sets are **2, 58, 200, 35 and 0** with seven, six, five, four and three gates remaining, respectively. The two initial sets differ only by inclusion of pin 5’s zero table. The certificate checks every gate choice and every possible reuse of an operand. An independent breadth-first search on all 21 unordered inputs also ended with an empty frontier after four gates.
