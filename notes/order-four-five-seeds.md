# An 82-gate search on fourteen exterior points

For L-meta-4197 the cyclic plane of order four has 21 points and 21 lines. Line ℓ contains the five point offsets 0, 1, 4, 14 and 16 from ℓ, with arithmetic modulo 21. An ordinary star circuit costs 125 gates. The posted case asks for 124.

We restore seven points, precisely the multiples of three, after a search on the remaining fourteen. The residual candidate must accept 70 prescribed flags and reject the indicated negative inputs in at most 82 gates. Its ordinary point stars cost 83. The seven restored stars, including their ORs into the residual output, take exactly 42 further gates. The [Lean attachment proof](FiveLineAttachment.lean) constructs that extension on the actual gate tapes and proves its value for all assignments. The appended gates compute four line ORs, the conjunction with the point, and the OR into the current result per restored point. The construction uses a valid residual output wire.

There is a short general certificate for the negative tests. If $k$ sets each have five elements and different sets intersect in at most one element, their union has at least $5k-\binom{k}{2}$ elements. When inserting a set, at most $k$ of its elements can already lie in the union of $k$ earlier sets. In particular any five distinct lines cover at least fifteen points.

Let $Z(X)$ be the lines avoiding a point set $X$, and $W(Y)$ be the points avoiding a line set $Y$. A maximal negative pair has the form $X,Z(X)$, where $X=W(Z(X))$. Every such $X$ is either a set of at most five points or $W(T)$ for a set of at most five lines. Indeed if $X$ has at least six points and $Z(X)$ has at most five lines, take $T=Z(X)$. Otherwise take any five lines in $Z(X)$. Their union avoids $X$ and already has at least fifteen of the 21 points. Its complement therefore equals $X$. The [Lean geometry proof](FiveLineCover.lean) proves the union estimate, checks the intersections for the actual cyclic plane, and derives this conclusion.

The [finite verifier](five-line-boundary.py) checks all 2,097,152 point subsets. There are 25,237 maximal negative pairs. The 27,896 subsets of size at most five, used once on each shore, yield 35,506 distinct tested point masks. Four-seed tests would miss 10,080 closed point masks with shore sizes five and five, and 168 with shore sizes six and six. The 83-gate exterior control passes all 70 required flags and all 25,237 maximal negatives. The restored 125-gate control passes all 105 flags and those same negatives. The 82-gate witness remains open.
