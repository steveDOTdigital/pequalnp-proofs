# Eleven monotone gates for graph mask 39251

L-meta-21137 asks for a circuit with at most ten gates. The minimum is **eleven** on the twenty promised inputs. The row neighborhoods in column order are {0,1,4}, {1,3}, {1,2} and {0}. Form the OR of each neighborhood, intersect with its row indicator, then take the OR of the four terms. This takes four column OR gates, four AND gates and three output OR gates. ## Excluding ten gates

Number a promised input by five times its row plus its column. With each successive indicator omitted, the following accepted-to-rejected pairs have componentwise increasing values on all remaining inputs.

| Omitted wire | r0 | r1 | r2 | r3 | c0 | c1 | c2 | c3 | c4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Accepted cell | 0 | 6 | 11 | 15 | 0 | 1 | 12 | 8 | 4 |
| Rejected cell | 5 | 16 | 16 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 10 | 5 | 2 |

Monotonicity therefore forces every indicator to feed the live output cone. Fold constants, copies and gates whose output is equal to an earlier still-available input signal on all promised inputs. The public gate convention permits empty, unary and out-of-range inputs. Those simplify to constants or copies. The target is nonconstant.

Evaluate a candidate circuit in gate order, retaining exactly the signals that have a future use, including the final output. Start with the nine raw indicators. A gate picks two available signals and computes their intersection or union as twenty-bit truth tables. An operand with a future use stays available. Each other operand is consumed. Thus every legal circuit yields a path through the search. Conversely, every path describes a valid sequence of binary gates. Permuting the available signals changes none of the possible continuations.

Two necessary conditions keep the search small. With m live signals and b gates left, b must be at least m−1, since a gate removes at most one signal on balance. Also, on each pair consisting of an accepted cell and a rejected cell, at least one live signal must have value one on the former and zero on the latter. Otherwise monotonicity forces the output at the accepted cell to be at most the output at the rejected cell.

The memoized [Python search](mask-39251-21137.py) exhausts the ten-gate budget, including all ways to retain zero, one or two operands. A separate breadth-first enumeration allowed duplicate and constant signals and obtained these counts of surviving unordered signal lists after zero through ten gates: 1, 102, 1,721, 6,965, 9,463, 4,812, 912, 69, 6, 0, 0. Neither search found the target at any earlier depth. Lean verifies the eleven-gate circuit against the public definition. The exhaustive lower bound has yet to be formalized in Lean.
