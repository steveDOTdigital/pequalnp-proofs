# Ten gates suffice for matrix 39161

[L-meta-21016](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-21016.ef689e9dd260.json) asks for a fan-in-two AND/OR circuit of at most nine gates on the one-per-side promise for a four-by-five matrix. Lean verified a ten-gate construction. A separate exhaustive search rules out nine. The lower bound currently has external computational verification.

The accepted pairs are the union of three rectangles: rows 1 and 2 with columns 1 and 2, row 0 with columns 3 and 4, and rows 0, 1 and 3 with column 0. Constructing their indicators takes three, two and three gates respectively. Two final OR gates give ten. [Lean construction on all 20 inputs](Mask39161Upper.lean)

The independent search represents each retained wire by its truth table on the 20 promised pairs. It starts with every subset of the nine input wires on which the output could depend. A monotone AND/OR circuit distinguishing an accepted input from a rejected one needs a retained wire that is true for the former and false for the latter. Each transition tries both binary operations and every way to keep or discard their operands after use. A cone with at most `b` binary gates remaining needs at most `b+1` retained inputs. Constants, projections and repeated operands can be removed from a circuit with a nonconstant output without increasing its size. These facts justify the two pruning rules and the reduced gate list.

With nine gates down to one left, the search reached **1, 47, 384, 955, 906, 332, 44, 4 and 0** states. The desired truth table appeared in none. The positive catalogue claim is therefore false according to the computational check. The independent upper and lower bounds resolve one statement.
