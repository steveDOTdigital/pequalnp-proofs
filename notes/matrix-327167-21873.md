# Nine gates for matrix 327167

On a one-row, one-column input the entries selected by the matrix have a short description. Row 0 or column 0 always suffices. Row 2 also accepts columns 1, 2 and 3. Row 1 also accepts columns 1, 2, 4 and 5. The two row tests share the gate for columns 1 or 2. This gives nine binary AND/OR gates. [Desk statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-21873.e0f7b294ef15.json)

For the lower bound, each live wire is a truth table on all 24 cells. With at most k gates left, at most k+1 wires can feed the output. For each accepting and rejecting cell, at least one live wire must be true on the accepting cell and false on the rejecting cell. Otherwise monotonicity rules out the required output. Enumerating binary AND/OR with operand reuse leaves respectively 1, 6, 13, 13, 6, 1 and 0 viable states with eight through two gates left.
