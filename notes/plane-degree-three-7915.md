# Bounded-degree test family for L-meta-7915

The [posted assignment](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-7915.c925f7b7aa14.json) uses the incidence relation `p - l ∈ {0, 1, 3, 9} mod 13`. In the branch with point degree at most two and line degree at most three, a family of 186 independent capped test pairs suffices. A capped pair selects no flag, and at most two selected lines pass through each point and at most two selected points lie on each line. Here degree means the syntactic bound from the circuit, computed by taking a maximum at OR and adding at AND.

If a monotone circuit accepts an independent capped pair, it accepts based on at most two point variables and at most three line variables from that input. At OR, choose one accepting predecessor. At AND, take the union of both predecessors' certificates. Unrolling shared gates for this argument preserves the recursive degrees. Lean checks this directly for the project's `Circuit`, `Monotone2`, `circuitFun` and `Circuit.lineDeg`. [Support proof](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/51e23717a164e41f1952a58d3077f7400ef5148b/notes/PlaneSupport7915.lean)

Write the smaller input as `(A, B)`. Extend `B` to a nonconcurrent triangle of lines `T` disjoint from `A`. At each choice the lines through the at most two points of `A` exclude at most eight lines. Once there are two lines, the pencil through their intersection excludes at most four more. The three sides of `T` miss four points. Through each missed point there is exactly one line that also avoids the three triangle vertices. Indeed, the other lines through the missed point join it to the three vertices. These four extensions are quadrangles with distinct omitted points in the exterior of `T`. At least two of their three-point exteriors contain `A`.

A family of 48 line quadrangles contains at most one extension of each triangle because any two members share at most two lines. Omit that family. Among the remaining 186 quadrangles, at least one exterior contains `A` and its line set contains `B`. The test uses that exterior and line set. Lean checks the packing and the abstract extension argument. The finite replay checks that each retained pair is capped and independent, and that every eligible small input is contained in a retained test. Lean checks separately that this coverage implies the circuit accepts every completion of one retained test whenever it accepts any independent capped pair in this degree branch. [Packing proof](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/72e4f6707a398435a4e7f98d54ba10065d2800ec/notes/PlaneDegreeThree7915.lean) · [Finite replay](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/8d5f3bb218e3902de32a29f7cd469d40f31f496c/notes/plane-degree-three-tests.mjs) · [Lean transport of coverage to circuits](https://github.com/steveDOTdigital/pequalnp-proofs/blob/beca02a943f409867001c4aa31fcf1a862e6d068/notes/PlaneTests7915.lean)

For this particular quadrangle-only construction, any covering family needs at least 182 tests. If two omitted quadrangles share a triangle, at most two of its four extensions remain. Select the missed point for each remaining extension as the point side and the triangle as the line side. No retained extension covers that input. Hence any omitted family has at most one occurrence of each triangle. For a fixed pair of lines, there are nine nonconcurrent triangles containing it. Each omitted quadrangle through that pair uses two of those triangles, so at most four omitted quadrangles contain the pair. There are 78 pairs and six pairs per quadrangle, giving at most 52 omissions from 234.

This reduces the negative examples needed in the line-degree-three branch. The gate lower bound for this branch and the posted branch with line degree at least four require separate arguments.
