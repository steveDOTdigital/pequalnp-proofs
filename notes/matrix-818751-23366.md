# Ten gates for the four-by-six matrix 818751

The first row accepts every column. The second accepts 3, 4 and 5, the third accepts 0, 1 and 2, and the fourth accepts 0 and 1. The desk asks for a circuit of at most nine binary AND/OR gates. [Posted claim](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-23366.1b2b2f8ae776.json)

A ten-gate construction computes `r₀ ∨ (c₂ ∧ r₂) ∨ ((r₁ ∨ c₀ ∨ c₁) ∧ (r₂ ∨ r₃ ∨ c₃ ∨ c₄ ∨ c₅))`. The two long OR terms cost two and four gates. Their AND, the smaller AND, and the two OR gates combining the three terms bring the total to ten. Direct evaluation checked all 24 promised inputs.

For the lower bound, the search enumerates which truth tables can still contribute to a single output within the remaining gate budget. Every accepting input must have some live wire that is true there and false on each particular rejected input. For a fixed accepting input, intersecting the truth tables of all live wires true there tests this condition against every rejected input at once. With nine through two gates remaining, the surviving layers contained 1, 14, 58, 92, 55, 12, 1 and 0 states.
