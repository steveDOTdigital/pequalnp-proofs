# Eight gates for the two-lit graph 35891

L-meta-23308 asks for at most seven gates. Eight suffice and seven are impossible. On the promised inputs, the output is the OR of three terms: both pins 2 and 3 are lit, pin 0 is lit while neither 2 nor 3 is lit, or pin 1 is lit together with either 4 or 5. This also handles repeated indices, where only one pin is lit. Lean checks all 36 ordered pairs, the gate list and fan-in. For the lower bound, list the 21 distinct promised inputs and encode each wire by its truth table. Normalize the output cone by folding constants, projections and repeated operands. Enumerate possible sets of initial inputs and process the gates in order. For each AND, OR or NOT, retain operands as needed for subsequent uses. With k gates left there can be at most k+1 live wires. At equality every remaining gate must consume two wires without retaining either, so monotonicity requires that each accepted input be separated from every rejected one by a wire true on the former and false on the latter. With more slack, distinguishability suffices as a necessary condition.

For budget seven the viable frontier sizes were 1, 28, 36, 27, 13 and 0. None contained the target. A separate depth-first search excluded seven and found the eight-gate construction.
