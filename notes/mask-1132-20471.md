# Seven gates for graph mask 1132

L-meta-20471 asks for a monotone circuit with at most six gates. The exact minimum is seven. The row neighborhoods are {2,3}, {0,1} and {0}. Form their three row terms and union them. The circuit uses two column ORs, three ANDs and two final ORs. [Posted statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-20471.7ce718d39a65.json) · [Lean witness](Mask1132Upper.lean)

All three row indicators and the four indicators of nonempty columns are essential. An omitted indicator's neighborhood would have to be contained in every other neighborhood on its side. This follows by comparing the accepted cells at that vertex to the same opposite vertex after selecting a different vertex. When the original indicator is unavailable, the remaining signal vector can only increase. All rows are nonempty and have empty common intersection. Column 4 is empty, so no nonempty column can be omitted.

A live cone with seven distinct raw sources and at most six binary gates must be a tree. Its connected underlying graph needs at least six plus the number of gates in edges, and each gate introduces at most two edges. Thus equality is forced, with no extra input, repeated wire or shared intermediate. Empty gates, constants and copies can be folded before this count. Combine sibling signals using bitwise AND or OR, consuming both arguments. Discard a state if some accepted input is componentwise below a rejected input on all surviving signals. Such a state cannot be completed by a monotone circuit.

The numbers of distinct viable unordered signal lists are 1 initially, then 8, 14, 6 and 0. None contains the target. This exhausts every possible six-gate tree. An independent depth-first search ruled out six and returned a different seven-gate construction. The finite lower bound is external to Lean. Run `python monotone-frontiers.py 3 5 1132 6`.
