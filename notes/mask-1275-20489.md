# Seven monotone gates for graph mask 1275

The posted six-gate upper bound is false. The exact minimum under the one-on-each-side promise is seven. The graph has neighborhoods {0,1,3,4}, {0,1,2} and {0} in order of row index. A seven-gate circuit computes the OR of c0, r1 AND (c1 OR c2), and r0 AND (c1 OR (c3 OR c4)). It has the required truth value on each of the fifteen promised inputs. [Posted statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-20489.17f5d0a66e71.json)

## Lower bound

Number a promised input by five times its row plus its column. For each raw indicator below, removing that indicator makes the remaining input vector increase componentwise between the two listed cells, even though the target falls from one to zero. A monotone circuit computing the target must therefore read that indicator in its live output cone.

| Indicator | Accepted cell | Rejected cell |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| r0 | 1 | 11 |
| r1 | 6 | 11 |
| c0 | 0 | 2 |
| c1 | 1 | 2 |
| c2 | 7 | 8 |
| c3 | 3 | 2 |
| c4 | 4 | 2 |

A connected cone with b gates and at least seven distinct input sources has at least b+6 edges. Each gate reads at most twice, so it has at most 2b edges. A budget of six forces equality. The cone is a tree, uses exactly the seven listed raw inputs, and every gate has exactly two children. A duplicated input edge or a shared intermediate would violate equality. The posted circuit convention permits constants and invalid references. Fold constant inputs and unary gates first, preserving the output and using no extra gates. This leaves a nonconstant cone to which the count applies.

Now consume sibling wires from the leaves upward. Start with the seven required raw indicators. For each unordered pair of available truth tables, replace both by their intersection or union. Drop a proposed state whenever an accepted cell's vector of remaining signals is componentwise at most a rejected cell's vector. The rest of the tree is monotone, so such a state can never produce the target. Sorting the signals preserves every possible next pairing. After successive merges the numbers of distinct surviving states are **4, 4, 1, 0**. Thus no sequence of six merges succeeds. An independent formula-size enumeration using all eight raw inputs also first reaches 1275 at size seven.

The [finite replay](mask-1275-20489.py) verifies the upper bound, indispensable inputs and all four search levels using Python's standard library. This is a mathematical disproof with a reproducible computation. The [Lean replay](Mask1275Finite.lean) kernel-checks the seven-gate witness against the public definition, the seven indispensable-input tests, and exhaustion after four merges. The structural argument that the live six-gate cone would be such a tree is written above. It has yet to be formalized in Lean.
