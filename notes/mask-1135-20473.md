# Six gates for graph 1135

The desk asks for five AND/OR gates. The exact minimum is six. The three row neighborhoods are {0,1,2,3}, {0,1} and {0}. One six-gate construction accepts column 0, column 1 on either of the first two rows, or columns 2 and 3 on row 0. [Posted claim](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-20473.eda36beac2a6.json) · [Lean proof of both bounds](Mask1135Exact.lean)

A short way to see the obstruction is to compare each accepted cell with each rejected cell. At least one live wire must be true on the first and false on the second. Otherwise no monotone continuation distinguishes them. Among sets of at most six raw wires, the only set meeting this condition is {r0, r1, c0, c1, c2, c3}. A five-gate output cone can use at most six inputs. At equality every gate consumes two wires and replaces them by one, so no computed signal or operand can be reused.

Only three possible first gates preserve all required comparisons: r1 OR c2, r1 OR c3, and c2 OR c3. After any of these, the only viable second step combines the result by OR with the remaining member of {r1,c2,c3}. The four remaining wires are now r0, c0, c1 and (r1 OR c2 OR c3). Every AND or OR of two of these that consumes both operands loses an accepted/rejected comparison. The target is absent at each depth.
