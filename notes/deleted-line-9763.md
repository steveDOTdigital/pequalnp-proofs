# The 58-gate question for the plane with one line removed

The exact circuit minimum for the assigned graph is between **39 and 59** in the ordinary AND/OR/NOT model. For monotone circuits the interval narrows to **40 through 59**. For monotone formulas the minimum is **59**. The search below also excludes every formula of at most 58 gates whose simplified signed expression has at most three variables appearing in both polarities. The unrestricted 58-gate question is still open. ### The 59-gate construction

Take thirteen points numbered modulo 13. Keep the twelve nonzero lines. Point p lies on the retained lines of indices p + d for d in {0, 4, 10, 12}, modulo 13. Line l has raw wire number 12 + l. We checked these labels against all 48 terms of the posted expression. For each line, form the OR of its four points and AND the result with that line's input. Twelve such terms use 48 gates and the final OR tree costs eleven. Lean checked the resulting circuit against the posted Boolean function on arbitrary inputs. The same file applies the earlier cross-rank theorem to prove that every monotone binary formula needs at least 60 leaves. All 48 flags must be covered, and no vertex covers more than four. In fact the only size-twelve vertex cover is the entire line shore. Omitting any nonempty set S of lines forces every point incident with S into the cover, and all 4,095 nonempty choices have more than |S| such points. ### Lower bounds allowing arbitrary sharing

Pinning a line low gives, up to relabeling, the 44-flag graph of L-meta-9792. The verifier checks the labeling for the first line and constructs 432 incidence automorphisms transitive on the twelve lines. The earlier restriction calculation gives at least 37 binary gates with free complements, and at least 38 in the monotone model, on the remaining graph. Every line is essential, so its deletion from a trimmed circuit costs at least one binary gate, contracting complements for the signed bound. A circuit with a NOT therefore needs at least 38 binary gates and one NOT. [Earlier restriction proof and verifier](punctured-plane-circuit.md) · [Geometry checks](deleted-line-9763-formulas.py)

Suppose a monotone circuit had at most 39 gates. Each line would have a single reader: two readers would both disappear on its low slice, leaving at most 37 gates. That reader has to be OR. A low pin at an AND makes it constant and simplifies a second gate at its consumer. The restricted output is nonconstant. As usual remove constants, aliases, repeated operands and dead gates first. Consider the earliest gate to read any line. If its other operand is a point-only signal, all points high make that signal true. With all other lines low the output must still distinguish both values of this line, which the sole OR reader cannot do. If the other operand is a different raw line, set it high, choose a point on the first line outside the second, and leave all other inputs low. The same contradiction follows. This proves the monotone lower bound of 40.

### Signed formulas

Push inversions to leaves and apply the earlier single-positive-occurrence simplification for a monotone target. Let m be the number of variables still occurring both positively and negatively. Each such variable needs two positive occurrences and at least one negative occurrence. The flag comparisons restrict which incident edge rows can be handled by each vertex. Each edge can be assigned an owning endpoint, with disjoint local marks. For every choice of mixed variables, the verifier enumerates independent sets of nonowners. Its local domains list the possible incident-edge ownership masks and their minimum read costs. The global search enforces complementary orientations at the two ends of each edge and a common leaf budget. It prunes a local choice only if the other endpoint has no compatible choice or the summed minimum costs already exceed budget. Integer enumeration uses all 29,854 nonowner sets, with symmetry only under the 432 explicitly checked graph automorphisms. [Comparison argument](heawood-signed-formulas.md) · [Weighted enumeration](deleted-line-9763-formulas.py)

For m equal to 0 or 1 the bound is at least 60 leaves. All 300 pairs and 2,300 triples require at least 59. A formula actually using NOT has to pay for at least one NOT in addition to its binary gates. For four mixed variables, the calculation excludes 12,038 of the 12,650 choices at a 58-leaf budget. For the other 612, the relaxed orientation constraints allow 58 leaves but rule out 57. These 612 cases do not supply a circuit. A 58-gate formula with four mixed variables would have to use exactly one NOT gate. At the other extreme, 17 or more mixed variables require at least 59 leaves just by occurrence counting. ### Direct search for a counterexample

Starting from a linear and a balanced 59-gate star construction, delete a gate by redirecting all its uses to any wire independent of that gate in the original DAG. Then replace one surviving output-cone gate by any binary AND/OR, single-input NOT or Boolean constant using any acyclic input choice in the edited DAG. We allow topological reordering after each edit. Comparing the 48 minimal positive inputs and 553 maximal independent inputs rejected every case. Including repeated operands, constants and equivalent edit descriptions, the two runs covered 1,303,796,948 and 1,536,355,160 replacement choices. Even a signed candidate must pass these rows, although passing them would only be sufficient for a monotone candidate. [Search replay](deleted-line-9763-search.mjs)

One further relation limits which constructions can save a gate. Restore the missing line with only three of its original incidences. Its three-neighbor star costs three gates, plus one OR to join it to the existing output. Thus an unrestricted 58-gate solution here would give a 62-gate solution for the 51-flag graph of case 9767. This extension adds no shared intermediate outputs. In particular, the earlier exclusions for monotone circuits sharing up to six same-shore raw OR pairs and any number of raw ANDs also exclude that route here. [51-flag case](plane-pair-9767.md)
