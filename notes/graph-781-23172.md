# Seven gates for graph 781

On the two-lit promise, this graph accepts the singleton 0 and the four pairs with one endpoint in {0, 1} and the other in {2, 3}. The posted claim asks whether six fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates suffice. Lean proved that the minimum is seven. [Desk statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-23172.8e72e0cec9b5.json) · [Lean proof](Graph781.lean)

A seven-gate circuit computes `(x₀ ∨ x₁) ∧ ((x₂ ∨ x₃) ∨ ¬(x₄ ∨ x₁ ∨ x₅))`. When the selected endpoint lies in {0, 1}, the middle pair of inputs accepts precisely the four cross-pairs. The negated term adds the singleton 0 without admitting the singleton 1, the pair {0, 1}, or a pair with 4 or 5. Both Lean and a separate calculation checked all 36 ordered inputs.

For the lower bound, encode each wire by its values on the 21 distinct singleton and pair inputs. For a circuit of at most six gates, enumerate the sets of wires that can still contribute to its output. At any stage these wires must distinguish every accepting input from every rejecting input. If all remaining gates are needed to combine wires, the suffix is monotone, so some wire must be true on each accepting input and false on the corresponding rejecting input. After these checks the layers contained 1, 2, 1 and 0 states with six through three gates left. The Lean kernel checked every transition before deriving the lower bound for the project's full circuit semantics. [Independent search and witness check](graph-781-search.py)
