# Eight gates for the four-by-four matrix 4458, with or without NOT

The desk asks whether seven fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates can represent this matrix on one-row, one-column inputs. Row 0 accepts columns 1 and 3. Row 1 accepts columns 1 and 2. Rows 2 and 3 each accept only column 0. [Desk statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-19075.0b7c16011b14.json)

A circuit forms the OR of three terms. They are row 0 AND (column 1 OR column 3), row 1 AND (column 1 OR column 2), and (row 2 OR row 3) AND column 0. The terms cost two gates apiece, and two more OR gates combine them. Lean checked all sixteen inputs.

The lower bound allows shared wires and NOT gates. Encode each wire by its truth table on the sixteen promised inputs. Any accepting input and any rejecting input must be distinguishable by at least one wire still needed to compute the output. With `b` gates remaining at most `b+1` wires can still contribute. When every gate must combine two wires, the remaining computation is monotone and the distinction must run from true to false. Enumerating all next gates and every possible reuse of their operands left 30, 749, 2,091, 1,034, 161, 17 and 0 states with seven down to one gates remaining. Lean independently checked a certificate that includes redundant transitions and uses sets of distinct wire values. Its counts were 30, 774, 2,091, 1,033, 161, 17 and 0. [Lean proof and construction](Matrix4458.lean) · [Independent replay](matrix-4458-search.py)

This refutes the assigned seven-gate statement and proves the separate [monotone eight-gate threshold](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-5555.425daa4231e6.json). The project had previously merged [tristan-hornetz’s proof of that threshold](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/11561).
