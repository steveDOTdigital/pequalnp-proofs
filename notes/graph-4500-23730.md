# Seven gates for graph 4500

The desk asks for a circuit of at most six fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT gates on five inputs. With either one or two inputs lit, the graph accepts {2}, {0, 2}, {1, 2}, {0, 4} and {1, 3}. [Desk statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-23730.c32969fd01ea.json)

The seven-gate circuit computes `(x₁ ∧ x₃) ∨ (x₀ ∧ x₄) ∨ (x₂ ∧ ¬(x₃ ∨ x₄))`. Its first two terms recognize one pair each. Its third accepts precisely the three remaining inputs. Lean checked every ordered pair, including repetitions, and an independent calculation reproduced all 25 values.

For the lower bound, represent each wire by its truth table on the fifteen distinct inputs. Retain only sets of live wires that distinguish every accepting input from every rejecting input and can still feed one output within the remaining gate budget. When each remaining gate must reduce the number of live wires, every remaining gate must be binary and monotone. In that case the distinguishing wire must be true on the accepted input and false on the rejected one. The independent search reached 1, 19, 4 and 0 states with six down to three gates left. The Lean certificate also checks redundant transitions and reached 1, 20, 4 and 0. Both exclude a six-gate circuit.
