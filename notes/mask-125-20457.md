# Seven gates for graph mask 125

L-meta-20457 asks for six monotone gates on a three-by-five graph. Its row neighborhoods are {0,2,3,4}, {0,1} and the empty set. The first two row terms use four and two gates respectively. Their OR gives a seven-gate witness, checked on all fifteen promised inputs in Lean. [Posted statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-20457.6fa69f2bf7bf.json) · [Lean witness](Mask125Upper.lean)

For the lower bound, fix column 4 to false, then column 3 to false. Each restriction removes at least one gate. In each case compare row 0 on the column being removed with row 0 on column 1. The first cell is accepted and the second rejected. If the removed indicator is omitted, all remaining raw inputs weakly increase, which contradicts monotonicity. That indicator must reach a live gate, since it alone is not the target. After fixing it to false, an AND reading it becomes false and an OR reading it becomes a copy or false. Constant and unary gates, including reads of nonexistent wires, can be folded. The second accepted-to-rejected pair survives the first restriction.

In the remaining three-by-three graph, number the rows and columns from zero. The seven cells (0,0), (0,1), (0,2), (1,0), (1,1), (1,2), (2,0) have target word 29 when the first listed cell occupies the low bit. These are exactly the seven cells used in the kernel-checked exclusion for graph 53. Its raw words are 7, 56, 64, 73, 18, 36 and 0. The three-by-three graph has the first six of these words. Any circuit on these six inputs can be simulated using the seven available words by ignoring the extra zero and shifting gate-output indices accordingly. Invalid indices still denote false. The prior exclusion rules out every monotone circuit with at most four gates even on these seven cells. A six-gate circuit for mask 125 would have produced such a circuit after the two deletions. A direct enumeration independently rules out six. The nonempty rows and all five columns are indispensable. Each omitted row or column has an accepted-to-rejected comparison in which all other inputs weakly increase. Thus a six-gate cone needs seven raw inputs, forcing it to be a tree. The counts of viable unordered signal lists after successive gate evaluations are 1, 7, 13, 8, 1 and 0. No target occurs. Run `python monotone-frontiers.py 3 5 125 6`.
