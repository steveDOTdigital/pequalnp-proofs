# Graph mask 294651 has monotone circuit size nine

L-meta-21854 asks for eight binary AND/OR gates under the one-on-each-side promise. Nine suffice and eight are impossible. Removing one of two identical rows and one of three identical columns gives the graph mask 1275, for which we previously established a seven-gate minimum. [Posted statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-meta-21854.be94d691b6cb.json) · [Seven-gate proof](mask-1275-20489.md)

For the upper bound, take the OR of c0, r2 AND (c1 OR c2), and (r0 OR r1) AND (c1 OR c3 OR c4 OR c5). There are nine binary gates. The [Lean verification](Mask294651Upper.lean) checks the exact 24 promised values and the restriction to mask 1275.

For the lower bound, ignore gates outside the live output cone, fold constants and remove copies. First delete row 0 by fixing r0 to false. This raw indicator is indispensable in the original circuit. Compare cell (row 0, column 1), which is accepted, to (row 3, column 1), which is rejected. Omitting r0 would make the observed inputs increase componentwise, contradicting monotonicity. The output is not r0, so some live binary gate reads r0. Setting r0 to false therefore eliminates at least that gate, by either making its result false or replacing its result by the other operand. Downstream simplifications can only reduce the gate count further.

Now delete column 5 from the resulting three-row circuit. The column indicator c5 is indispensable even after deleting row 0. In original row 1, column 5 is accepted while column 2 is rejected. Omitting c5 again makes the observed inputs increase. The restricted output is not c5, so fixing c5 to false removes at least one more binary gate. Relabeling the remaining rows and columns changes no gate count. This gives a circuit for mask 1275 with at least two fewer gates. An eight-gate circuit for mask 294651 would thus yield at most six gates for mask 1275, contradicting the seven-gate minimum.

A separate direct enumeration starts from the nine indispensable inputs r0,r1,r2,c0,c1,c2,c3,c4,c5. A live cone with nine sources and just eight gates must be a read-once tree. Applying the sibling-merge and monotonicity test from the seven-gate proof gives 9, 26, 28, 10, 1 and then 0 surviving states. This independently rules out eight gates without using the restriction. The full lower bound is a written proof supported by finite enumeration.
